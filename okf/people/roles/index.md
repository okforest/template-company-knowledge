# Roles

* [CEO](ceo.md) - Chief Executive Officer: sets direction and owns the company's mission, vision, and values.
* [CFO](cfo.md) - Chief Financial Officer: owns budgets, pricing, billing, and accounting.
* [CMO](cmo.md) - Chief Marketing Officer: owns the brand, the market, and marketing.
* [CRO](cro.md) - Chief Revenue Officer: owns sales and customer support.
* [COO](coo.md) - Chief Operating Officer: owns operations, people, and legal.
* [CTO](cto.md) - Chief Technology Officer: owns the company's systems and how they fit together.
* [CISO](ciso.md) - Chief Information Security Officer: owns security, access, incident response, and the secrets policy.
